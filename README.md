# 🟨🟥 Catalan Verb Lab | C1–C2

### An Interactive Learning Platform for Advanced Catalan Grammar

**Catalan Verb Lab** is an interactive educational resource designed to help advanced learners practise and master Catalan verb conjugations, with a particular focus on irregular patterns, phonetic alternations, and complex verb forms.

The project combines progressive learning, interactive exercises, and immediate feedback to support independent study and exam preparation at **CEFR levels C1–C2**.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Identify and apply regular and irregular conjugation patterns.
- Distinguish between pure and inchoative third-conjugation verbs.
- Practise vowel alternations and velarized verb stems.
- Master irregular participles and complex subjunctive forms.
- Develop accuracy through contextualized exercises and complete conjugation tasks.

## 📚 Learning Structure

The platform contains **10 progressive learning units and 100 exercises**, covering:

1. Regular conjugation patterns
2. Pure third-conjugation verbs
3. Inchoative verbs (*-eix-*)
4. Vowel alternations (*o/u*)
5. Velarized verb stems
6. Irregular verb roots
7. Irregular participles
8. Advanced conjugation practice
9. Complete verb conjugations
10. Verbs in real-life contexts

## 🧩 Interactive Features

- Multiple-choice questions
- Written conjugation exercises
- Matching activities
- Full six-person conjugation challenges
- Contextualized sentence completion
- Automatic correction and immediate feedback
- Progress tracking

The interface uses large typography and clearly highlighted verb tenses and grammatical persons to improve readability and reduce avoidable mistakes.

## 🎨 Design

The visual identity draws inspiration from Catalan culture, incorporating the red and yellow colours of the *senyera* and Barcelona's traditional *panot* pavement motif.

## 💻 Technologies

- CSS3

- GitHub Pages

The application runs directly in a web browser without external dependencies or installation.
